HealthMCP User Agreement

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This agreement applies to anyone who authorizes HealthMCP, a personal, self-hosted app operated by Assawin Boontep, to read their Huawei Health activity data. By authorizing HealthMCP on Huawei's authorization page, you agree to these terms.

1. The service

HealthMCP reads activity and exercise data from Huawei Health through the HUAWEI Health Kit RESTful API and shows summaries and trends of that data. It is a personal, non-commercial project and is provided free of charge.

2. Your consent

HealthMCP only reads the data types you allow on Huawei's authorization page. It never writes or changes data in Huawei Health. You can withdraw consent at any time in your HUAWEI ID account settings or in the Huawei Health app.

3. Not medical advice

HealthMCP is for personal reference only. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease, and it must not be used in place of advice from a doctor or other qualified professional.

4. Your responsibilities

Only authorize HealthMCP with your own HUAWEI ID, and do not try to access data belonging to other people.

5. Availability

HealthMCP is provided "as is". It may be unavailable, changed or stopped at any time, and data shown may be delayed or incomplete compared with the Huawei Health app.

6. Privacy

How data is handled is described in the HealthMCP Privacy Policy.

7. Changes

If this agreement changes, the date above will be updated.
